## How to use these notes

- **Read online:** open the link above on your laptop or phone.
- **Save as PDF:** open a phase in Chrome → `Cmd/Ctrl + P` → *Save as PDF* → under *More settings* turn on **Background graphics**. All quiz answers are included in the PDF.
- **Track progress:** tick the *Got it* box at the end of each chapter. Your ticks are saved in your own browser.
